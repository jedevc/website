## Method

### Caramelise the onions

Heat a splash of olive oil in a large pan over medium-low heat. Add the sliced
onions with a pinch of salt and cook slowly, stirring occasionally, until deeply
golden and soft. Stir in the garlic and cook until fragrant. Set aside, then
wipe the pan clean for the cheese sauce.

{{< message warning >}}
**Give the onions time.** The longer they cook gently, the deeper the flavour.
{{< /message >}}

### Make the cheese sauce

In the same pan, melt the butter over medium heat. Add the flour and stir
constantly to cook out the raw flour taste. Gradually add the milk a splash at a
time, whisking well between each addition to avoid lumps, then pour in the
remainder and whisk until smooth. Continue
stirring until the sauce thickens enough to coat the back of a spoon.

Reduce the heat to low and stir in the Dijon mustard. Add the cheese a handful
at a time, stirring until
melted and fully incorporated before adding more. Taste and adjust with
salt and black pepper, then fold the onions and garlic back through
the sauce.

### Cook the pasta

Bring a large pot of generously salted water to a rolling boil. Add the pasta
and cook until al dente, about a minute less than the packet
suggests. Drain well.

### Assemble and bake

Preheat the oven. In a large baking dish, combine the drained pasta with the
cheese sauce and stir until every piece is coated. Arrange the tomatoes in a
single, slightly overlapping layer on top. Bake until
the sauce is bubbling and the tomatoes have softened.

Remove from the oven, scatter the breadcrumbs evenly over the top, along with
the Parmesan if using, then return to the oven until the crumb is golden and
crisp. Finish with a generous scattering of parsley and serve immediately.

### Cheat's tomato chutney

Place a punnet of cherry tomatoes in a microwave-safe bowl with a little olive
oil and a heavy pinch of salt. Cover tightly and microwave on high, stirring
halfway through. Balance with brown sugar, honey, or maple syrup if needed; hot
sauce works well for heat.
